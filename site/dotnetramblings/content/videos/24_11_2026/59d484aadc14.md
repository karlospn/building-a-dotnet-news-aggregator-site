Allie Laabs, Founding Developer Advocate at TypeSafe AI, explains why the company calls its model Jev a System One model, after Daniel Kahneman's term for the quick, intuitive judgments a knowledgeable person makes in seconds. With a customer support routing example, she shows how putting a whole decision into one Jev question leads to prompt gymnastics. Her alternative is to ask Jev only the small, fuzzy questions…
