Every mock is a guess about how an AWS service behaves. In this video I show what happens when the guess is wrong, and how to catch it on your own computer and in CI. The sample app is a small pub game on Amazon SQS and Amazon DynamoDB. Customers order, the bartender serves, and the order is deleted from the queue. I start with mocks. An agent writes a fake SQS and a fake DynamoDB, and the tests pass on my machine…
