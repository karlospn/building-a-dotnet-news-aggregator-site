Microsoft has released a public preview of dotnetup, a cross-platform toolchain manager that installs and updates .NET SDKs and runtimes in the user’s own directory without admin rights. It tracks channels such as latest, lts, preview, and daily, reads global.json, and removes unused installs. Commands may still change before general availability. By Almir Vuk
