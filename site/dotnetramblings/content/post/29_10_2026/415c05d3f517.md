NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL is a 120B-parameter mixture-of-experts model that pairs reasoning and tool use with multimodal understanding. This post explores its architecture and use cases, then shows how to deploy it on Amazon EKS, EKS Auto Mode, and Amazon SageMaker HyperPod EKS with NVIDIA Dynamo and vLLM.
