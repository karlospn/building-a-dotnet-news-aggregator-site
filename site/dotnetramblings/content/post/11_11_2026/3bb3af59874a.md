AWS Marketplace now supports usage-based metering for Amazon SageMaker bidirectional streaming inference, so model providers can charge for what customers actually consume during a live stream. Deepgram is the launch partner, bringing usage-based speech-to-text and text-to-speech billing to AWS Marketplace.
