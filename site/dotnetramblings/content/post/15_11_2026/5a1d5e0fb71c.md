Connection pooling significantly enhances database operation efficiency by reusing existing connections, reducing setup time. In SQLite benchmarks, pooling achieved up to an 11× speedup under heavy concurrency. While beneficial for applications with frequent connections, pooling should be enabled by default, with exceptions for specific cases requiring immediate connection release.
