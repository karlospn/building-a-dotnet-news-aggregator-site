DevFlow is a set of developer tools for connecting to and inspecting a running .NET MAUI app through the CLI and browser-based Inspector. I love it because it gives agents a way to run, navigate, interact with, and validate the app while it is running, making it much easier to debug and understand what is happening on an emulator or device.
