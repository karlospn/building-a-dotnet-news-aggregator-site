Take a big processing job, cut it into small steps, connect the steps with queues. Each step deploys, scales and gets reused on its own. That's Pipes and Filters, and you've used it even if you never named it: every cat | grep | sort in a shell is one. In practice, you'll have small steps, connected by queues.
