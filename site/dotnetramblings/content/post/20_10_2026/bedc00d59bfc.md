The third post in our semantic-layer series builds a governed customer 360 model on Amazon Redshift with AtScale, then exposes it to a Strands agent on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore through the AtScale MCP server. Identity propagation, RBAC, row security, and full query audit are enforced at the semantic layer, not the prompt.
