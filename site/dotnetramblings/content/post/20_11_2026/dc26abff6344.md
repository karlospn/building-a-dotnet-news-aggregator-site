Standard Bank Group automated the evidence behind its generative AI approval gates. Working with AWS Professional Services, the bank built an assessment in its own AWS account that scores each use case against international AI standards and its own policies using LLM-as-a-judge on Amazon Bedrock, giving risk committees consistent, traceable evidence.
