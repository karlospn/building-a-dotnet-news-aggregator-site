Logging is crucial for app monitoring, extending beyond exceptions to include diagnostic events like request timing and connection details. Spargine offers three HTTP listeners—HttpEventListener, HttpEventListenerAsyncLocal, and HttpRequestsObserver—each providing various levels of detail to help troubleshoot performance issues and improve logging for .NET applications.
