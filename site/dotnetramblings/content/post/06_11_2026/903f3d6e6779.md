Part 2 scales the pattern to an enterprise: Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Gateway becomes the single front door and redeems Okta's grant, Cedar policies bind each tool to the agent, a second agent runs on Amazon EKS, and AgentCore Observability traces every allow and deny beside Okta's log in Amazon CloudWatch.
