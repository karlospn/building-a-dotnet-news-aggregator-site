Multi-turn agentic workloads reprocess growing conversation context each turn, driving up latency. See how bi-directional KV cache transfer lets prefill and decode nodes reuse previously computed context across turns, cutting time to first token by up to 1.6x for Qwen3-32B and 3.3x for GLM-5-FP8 on Amazon EC2 P5en instances with EFA.
