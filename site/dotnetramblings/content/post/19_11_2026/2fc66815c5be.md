Amazon SageMaker AI now monitors your generative AI inference endpoints by default and tells you when a change could improve performance or lower cost. It tracks latency, GPU utilization, and serving configuration, then sends a finding through AWS Health with a one-click path to benchmark alternatives in SageMaker Studio before you apply any change.
