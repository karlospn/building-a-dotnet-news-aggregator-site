Running Amazon Bedrock across many accounts shows you the bill but not what happened inside each call. This post builds a centralized observability data lake that aggregates Bedrock invocation logs from every linked account into one queryable table, so you can see prompts, token counts, model choice, and per-invocation cost across your organization.
