When you deploy an agent, whether it is working is really several questions: did it finish the task, stay on topic, leak data, or skip steps? This post shows how to evaluate agents on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore with built-in, DeepEval, and AutoEval evaluators, and how to match each behavior that matters to the right evaluator at the right level.
