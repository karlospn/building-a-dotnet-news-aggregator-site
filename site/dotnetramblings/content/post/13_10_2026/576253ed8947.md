ShipStation Global's engineering team built Deckhand, an AI IT help desk agent for Slack on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore. It answers routine IT questions from a curated knowledge base, reads error-message screenshots, and files Jira tickets. In its first three months it resolved 97.5% of channel queries with a 96% positive feedback rating.
