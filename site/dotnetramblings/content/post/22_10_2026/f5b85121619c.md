Amazon SageMaker AI training session APIs let you write your own LLM training loop (SFT, RL, or custom objectives) in a Python script while the service runs the GPUs. This post introduces the programming model and three SDK clients, then walks through a GRPO loop on GSM8K and a reinforcement learning workload on competitive programming.
