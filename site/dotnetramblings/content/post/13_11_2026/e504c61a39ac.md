Amazon SageMaker HyperPod Data Capture records live inference inputs and outputs to Amazon S3. This post walks through one end-to-end example: capturing a support router's production traffic, surfacing a new-product blind spot with Amazon Athena, fine-tuning on the captured data with the HyperPod training operator, and measuring the before-and-after lift.
