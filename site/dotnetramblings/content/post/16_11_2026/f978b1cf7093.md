With Denied Topics in Amazon Bedrock Guardrails, you block a content category by describing it in plain language, and the guardrail matches by meaning rather than keyword, which can block more than you intend. This post presents eight best practices for writing a topic precisely enough that the guardrail blocks what you mean and passes what you don't.
