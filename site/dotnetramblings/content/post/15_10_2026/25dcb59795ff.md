Enterprise leaders want AI agents to take on more work but don't fully trust them to act correctly every time. Trust in Depth is AWS's layered approach-scope, know, bound, observe, and prove-that lets agents act on their own when their actions can be verified, reserving human judgment for the decisions that need it.
