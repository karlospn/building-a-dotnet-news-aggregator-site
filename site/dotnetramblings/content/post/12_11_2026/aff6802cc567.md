Agents stall between demo and release when teams lack a repeatable way to measure how good an agent is and whether the last change helped. This post covers three agent properties that break traditional CI/CD, a measured case where an aggregate metric hides a coverage gap, and Agent DLC: one lifecycle, two loops, and one explicit standard for judging every change.
