Comparing per-token prices hides the real cost of running a model on Amazon Bedrock. This post shows how to calculate the true cost per task across four hidden cost drivers, reduce it with caching, routing, and output constraints, and prove agent ROI with human baselines, holdout experiments, and a five-dimension scorecard.
