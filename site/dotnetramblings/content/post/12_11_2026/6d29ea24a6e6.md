The migration guide in the amazon-bedrock skill helps you migrate a customer-managed Amazon Bedrock knowledge base to a Managed Knowledge Base. Your coding agent plans the move with read-only discovery, creates the new knowledge base or an infrastructure-as-code template alongside the original, and validates retrieval before you cut over.
