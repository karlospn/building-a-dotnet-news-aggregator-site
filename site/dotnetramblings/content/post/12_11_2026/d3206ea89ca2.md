Build a composable, interoperable multi-agent system on AWS with the Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol. A LangChain orchestrator on Amazon ECS communicates with a Strands agent on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore across organizational boundaries, using bidirectional, OAuth-authenticated calls, with neither side depending on the other's framework or SDK.
