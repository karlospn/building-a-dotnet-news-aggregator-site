Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Identity now supports private certificate authorities (CAs), so your agents can connect to self-hosted identity providers that use a private CA. This post shows how to issue an IdP certificate with AWS Private CA, configure a credential provider to trust it, rotate the CA, and protect the certificate.
